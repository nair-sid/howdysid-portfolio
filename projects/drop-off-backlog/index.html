The Drop-Off Backlog

This board scores six onboarding friction points from Indian lending apps using RICE, live. Move any slider — Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort — and the backlog re-ranks itself.

These six came from real user complaints, not guesswork: Play Store reviews, and threads scraped from Reddit and Quora where people explain exactly why they abandoned a lending app mid-onboarding. Out of everything found, these are the six friction points that show up most often as the reason someone didn't go through with it.

FYIThe six problems are real, the R·I·C·E numbers are illustrative. The point of this artifact is the mechanism — move a slider and watch RICE reprioritize the backlog.

RICE = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort
Reach — users/month Impact — 0.25 to 3 Confidence — % Effort — person-weeks
M Must have — ships this round, non-negotiable S Should have — important, survivable to skip once C Could have — nice if there's time, cut first W Won't have — parked, not this round
