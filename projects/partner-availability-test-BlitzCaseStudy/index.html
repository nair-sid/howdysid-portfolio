The Partner Availability Test

Cab apps show how many cars are nearby before you book. Checkout for same-day delivery shows nothing about whether the order can move fast. This is the test for whether that same signal earns trust here too.

This project shows how I'd turn a product gap into a real A/B test — not just the idea, but a hypothesis worth shipping.
